### 2016 — 5 Neuroscience Scholars
*(Source: prnewswire.com/news-releases/exceptional-early-career-scientists-named-pew-scholars-in-the-biomedical-sciences-300280288.html + individual pew.org pages)*

| Year | Full Name | Institution | Research Area |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| 2016 | Martha W. Bagnall | Washington University School of Medicine | vestibular/spinal circuits, motor control |
| 2016 | Marco Gallio | Northwestern University | thermosensory neural circuits (Drosophila) |
| 2016 | Roozbeh Kiani | NYU Langone Medical Center | neural circuits for decision-making |
| 2016 | Dragana Rogulja | Harvard Medical School | sleep circuits, sensory gating |
| 2016 | Michael M. Yartsev | University of California, Berkeley | vocal learning neural mechanisms (bats) |

**Bagnall:** "investigate how the nervous system senses spatial orientation and routes this information through the spinal cord." (pew.org/…/2016/martha-bagnall)
**Gallio:** "neural mechanisms that guide fruit flies toward an environment of a preferred temperature…brain circuits that process temperature information shaping a variety of behaviors." (pew.org/…/2016/marco-gallio)
**Kiani:** "neural circuits that enable us to make decisions and flexibly adjust our behavior…large-scale recordings in the monkey brain." (pew.org/…/2016/roozbeh-kiani)
**Rogulja:** "identifying genes and neural circuits that gate sensory information during sleep, and…how gating changes with age." (pew.org/…/2016/dragana-rogulja)
**Yartsev:** "investigate the neural mechanisms that allow bats to learn their vocal communication signals…monitoring the activities of neurons in freely flying and behaving fruit bats." (pew.org/…/2016/michael-yartsev)

### 2017 — 5 Neuroscience Scholars
*(Source: pew.org/en/about/news-room/press-releases-and-statements/2017/06/pew-awards-22-promising-biomedical-researchers-funding-to-advance-human-health + individual pew.org pages)*

| Year | Full Name | Institution | Research Area |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| 2017 | Eiman Azim | Salk Institute for Biological Studies | spinal/brain motor circuits, skilled movement |
| 2017 | Michael A. Crickmore | Boston Children's Hospital | motivation/behavior neural circuits (Drosophila) |

### 2018 — 5 Neuroscience Scholars
*(Source: pew.org/en/about/news-room/press-releases-and-statements/2018/06/14/pew-selects-22-scientists-to-investigate-fundamental-biomedical-questions + individual pew.org pages)*

| Year | Full Name | Institution | Research Area |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| 2018 | Hiroyuki Kato | University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill | auditory neural circuits and behavior |
| 2018 | Nuo Li | Baylor College of Medicine | working memory/decision brain circuits |
| 2018 | Kassandra Ori-McKenney | University of California, Davis | tau, neurodegeneration after brain injury |
| 2018 | Erin L. Rich | Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai | expectation/reward-based brain circuits |
| 2018 | José A. Rodriguez | University of California, Los Angeles | prion aggregate structure, neurodegeneration |

**Kato:** "explore how mice extract and process auditory information from the environment…how specific subregions in the mouse brain respond to complex sound features." (pew.org/…/2018/hiroyuki-kato)
**Nuo Li:** "how multiple parts of the brain cooperate to guide decisions based on short-term memory…dissecting the neural circuits involved in such behavior." (pew.org/…/2018/nuo-li)
**Ori-McKenney:** "study the mechanisms that trigger dementia and neurodegeneration following traumatic brain injury…tau phosphorylation…DYRK1a also contributes to neurological diseases such as Alzheimer's." (pew.org/…/2018/kassandra-ori-mckenney)
**Rich:** "exploring how the brain uses expectations to motivate behavior…neural activity in a network of key brain areas…disorders that involve inappropriate or inflexible expectations such as anxiety, depression, and substance abuse." (pew.org/…/2018/erin-l-rich)
**Rodriguez:** "study the structure of toxic prion aggregates, a form of infectious protein that causes neurodegenerative disorders such as Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease." (2018 press release, pew.org)
